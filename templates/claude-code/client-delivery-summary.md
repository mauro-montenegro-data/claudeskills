# Client Delivery Summary Template

Client: [CLIENT]
Project: [PROJECT]
Reporting period: [DATE_RANGE]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]

Turn technical implementation details into a client-ready update.

## Scope
- Input notes/artifacts: [INPUTS]
- In scope: delivered work, current status, risks, next steps.
- Out of scope: deep internal engineering detail.

## Constraints
- Keep tone clear, factual, and non-defensive.
- Translate technical items into business impact.
- Explicitly call out open dependencies and owners.

## Expected output
1. Delivery summary (what was completed).
2. Current status and impact.
3. Known risks or blockers.
4. Next actions with owners and dates.
5. Decisions needed from client (if any).

## Review checklist
- [ ] Understandable for non-technical stakeholders.
- [ ] No hidden blockers.
- [ ] Dates/owners are explicit.
- [ ] Scope completed vs pending is clear.
